Companies

The fictional companies behind the cases. Each has its own industry, size and team, and its problems follow from them.

Furniture3 cases

Ardıç Mobilya

Makes living room sets and bedroom suites and sells them through 64 dealers. Annual revenue is 540 million TL, and 10% of it comes from a single dealer in Ankara.

Kayseri · 160 employees · 64 dealers, 1,500 deliveries a month
Construction3 cases

Atabey Yapı

A construction company that builds middle-income housing projects in Antalya. It sells most of its apartments while construction is under way, in installments and at a fixed price, and hires subcontractors for work such as the rough construction and insulation.

Antalya · 60 employees · About 180 homes a year, 3 construction sites
E-commerce3 cases

Atölye Nar

A small brand that designs handmade silver jewelry and gifts and makes them in its own workshop. Of its monthly revenue of 1.9 million TL, 80% comes from a social media account with 212,000 followers and the rest from its own website.

Izmir · 12 employees · 1,900 orders a month
Education3 cases

Bilgi Çınarı Kursları

An educational institution founded by a math teacher, offering high school and university exam prep courses at three branches in Eskişehir. The annual course fee is 96,000 TL; more than half of its students are returning from the previous year.

Eskişehir · 48 employees · 3 branches, 1,150 students
Manufacturing3 cases

Demirtaş Döküm

A second-generation family company that produces ductile iron and gray iron castings for the automotive and agricultural machinery industries. 65% of output is ductile iron casting; most customers are automotive OEMs and tier suppliers.

Konya · 140 employees · 900 tons a month, two shifts
Food & Drink3 cases

Demli Kahve

A nine-branch coffee chain that roasts its own beans. It runs four branches itself; the other five are with operators who are granted the brand name in exchange for 6% of revenue.

Ankara · 80 employees · 9 branches
Food & Drink3 cases

Fırın Sokak

A neighborhood restaurant chain serving pide, clay-pot casseroles and home-style dishes. Monthly revenue is 14 million TL; most of the prep is done in a central kitchen and distributed to the branches.

Izmir · 120 employees · 6 branches, central kitchen
Retail3 cases

Güneşli Market

A local supermarket chain with 14 neighborhood stores in Bursa and monthly revenue of about 95 million TL. Known for its butcher counter, produce section and deli, it also sells some staples under its own “Güneşli Sofra” label.

Bursa · 320 employees · 14 stores
Logistics3 cases

Hızır Kurye

Makes same-day deliveries in Istanbul by motorcycle courier for online stores and corporate customers. 112 of its 130 couriers are self-employed; monthly revenue is about 11.5 million TL.

Istanbul · 152 employees · 130 couriers, 5,200 deliveries a day
Software3 cases

Kodmer Yazılım

Sells pre-accounting software by monthly subscription to tradespeople and small businesses: invoicing, customer accounts, inventory and cash tracking. The average monthly subscription fee is 950 TL; the software is written by a nine-person development team.

Ankara · 34 employees · 5,200 subscriber businesses
Tourism3 cases

Mavi Koy Otel

A family-run boutique hotel right on the sea in Fethiye, open from April to the end of October. 41 of its 55 employees are seasonal; 45 percent of the rooms are allocated to a single foreign tour company.

Muğla · 55 employees · 64 rooms

Ankara · 28 employees · 9 service engineers, 640 installed devices

Energy3 cases

Solvera Enerji

Builds solar power plants on factory rooftops, handling design, installation and subsequent maintenance with its own crew. It completes roughly 30 megawatts of installations a year; most of its customers are industrial companies in and around Konya.

Konya · 36 employees · 30 megawatts installed per year

Automotive3 cases

Usta Oto Servis

An independent garage in Adana with three branches that maintains and repairs vehicles of every make. About 1,200 work orders are opened each month; the average work order is 6,500 TL, and annual revenue is around 94 million TL.

Adana · 42 employees · 3 branches, 1,200 vehicles a month
Logistics3 cases

Vargel Lojistik

Provides full-truckload and part-load road freight for automotive suppliers and fast-moving consumer goods companies. The fleet runs at 93% utilization, and a third of revenue comes from a single automotive customer.

Kocaeli · 85 employees · 42 tractor units, 3 warehouses
Food3 cases

Yaylacık Süt Ürünleri

A family business that makes white cheese, kashar and yogurt from milk collected from 186 farmers in the villages around Balıkesir. 45% of its output goes to a single supermarket chain, and the rest to delis, grocers and restaurants in the region.

Balıkesir · 75 employees · 40 tons of milk a day, 186 farmers
