Shipping prices went up: free shipping costs 176,000 euros a year

Savonnerie Clairvent E-commerce · Lyon, France · 14 employees · 62,000 orders a year
The problem

44% of orders clear the free-shipping threshold, and the carrier has raised its price to 6.45 euros a parcel. Raising the threshold could cut sales.

How it was solved

It left the free-shipping threshold at 35 euros for pickup points and raised it to 49 euros for home delivery. In six months the cost of free shipping fell from 87,978 euros to 61,668 euros, and the number of orders did not change.

What would you do? 3 comments ↓

What happened?

Clairvent doesn't charge for shipping on orders of 35 euros and over. In January the carrier raised its price per parcel from 5.90 euros to 6.45 euros. Of the 62,000 orders a year, 44%, or 27,280 parcels, go free; at the new price the annual cost comes to nearly 176,000 euros. The extra burden from the increase alone is 15,000 euros a year.

Founder Camille Vasseur wanted to raise the threshold to 45 euros. Nadia Belkacem, Head of E-commerce, objected: the average basket was 31 euros, and a significant share of customers added one more soap to their basket precisely to get past 35 euros. In February a two-week test was run; half the visitors were shown a threshold of 45 euros. In that group the share of visitors who went on to order fell from 2.9% to 2.5%.

Solène Guichard, Head of Customer Service and Logistics, pointed to another figure. The same carrier charged 4.10 euros when it took the parcel to a pickup point rather than the home. But with free shipping the customer wasn't asked about this option; the parcel went straight to their home. Nobody knew what share of customers would accept a pickup point.

By the numbers

Orders per year 62,000; those with free shipping 44% (27,280 parcels)
Shipping per parcel: home delivery 6.45 euros (previously 5.90), pickup point 4.10 euros
Free-shipping threshold 35 euros, average basket 31 euros
Conversion with a 45 euro threshold: from 2.9% to 2.5%

What did Savonnerie Clairvent do?

At the start of March two thresholds took effect: orders over 35 euros were shipped free to a pickup point, and those over 49 euros free to the home. Nadia Belkacem explained the change on the site's home page and on the basket page. In the first three weeks Solène Guichard received 46 messages saying “it used to be free”; then they stopped.

From March to the end of August 31,000 orders were shipped, almost the same as in the same period of the previous year. The share of orders with free shipping fell from 44% to 41%: 12,710 parcels. Of these, 8,643 went to pickup points and 4,067 to homes. Under the old system, 13,640 parcels would have gone free to the home in the same period, costing 87,978 euros; under the new one, the cost of free shipments was 61,668 euros. In six months 26,310 euros were saved. The average basket rose from 31 euros to 33.50 euros.

What to do in a similar situation, step by step

1First 24 hours

  • Trust the result of the test and don't raise the threshold to 45 euros for everyone. The drop in conversion is bigger than what you would save on shipping.
  • Stop thinking of free shipping as one thing. What you promise the customer is that the parcel comes free; getting it to the door is a separate service and a separate cost.

2This week

  • Set two thresholds: keep the threshold the same for pickup-point delivery and raise it for home delivery. The price-sensitive customer loses nothing, and the one who wants delivery to the door pays the difference through their basket.
  • Announce the change on the site openly: what is changing, what stays the same, and why. A customer who meets a surprise on the basket page abandons the order.
  • On the basket page, show the customer how far they are from the threshold and suggest low-priced products.
  • Track the figures weekly for the first month: number of orders, average basket, share of delivery types. If orders fall, leave yourself a way back.

3Lasting fix

  • Monitor shipping cost per order every month, not once a year when the price rise arrives.
  • When you renew the contract with the carrier, make pickup-point volume a bargaining point; the more volume you shift there, the more you can ask for on price.
  • Review the free-shipping threshold together with the average basket. Leaving the threshold where it is as baskets grow means sending a few more parcels for free every year.
The lesson

Before taking back a promise made to the customer, check whether there is a cheaper way to keep it.
