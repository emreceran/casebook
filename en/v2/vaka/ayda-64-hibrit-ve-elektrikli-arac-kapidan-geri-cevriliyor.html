64 hybrid and electric vehicles a month are turned away at the door

Usta Oto Servis Automotive · Adana · 42 employees · 3 branches, 1,200 vehicles a month
The problem

The mechanics have no high-voltage training and don't touch these vehicles. The number of vehicles turned away has tripled in six months.

How it was solved

Instead of equipping all three branches, it made Merkez the specialist branch and trained two mechanics. Vehicles turned away fell from 64 to 9 a month.

What would you do? 3 comments ↓

What happened?

At Usta Oto Servis's three branches, hybrid and electric vehicles are being turned away at the door. It was 22 vehicles a month in April and 64 in September. With the average work order at 6,500 TL, 416,000 TL a month of work is going to other garages. Some of the turned-away customers now take their gasoline car at home somewhere else as well.

General Manager Ozan Gündoğdu wants to equip all three branches. Insulated tool sets, diagnostic equipment and dedicated work areas require 1.35 million TL, and five-day high-voltage training for four mechanics costs 38,000 TL each; the total is about 1.5 million TL.

Founder Cemal Gündoğdu objects: “We're engine men. Those cars are the authorized dealer's business; they won't come to us until the warranty runs out.” Shop Foreman Şükrü Bayram has a different reservation: the mechanics are afraid of high voltage, and a mechanic who gets trained may leave a month later for a place that pays more.

By the numbers

Vehicles turned away: 22 in April, 64 in September
Lost work: 416,000 TL a month (6,500 TL per work order)
Equipment for three branches 1.35 million TL, training 38,000 TL per person
Number of mechanics with training: 0

What did Usta Oto Servis do?

When the front desk records were reviewed, 41 of the 64 turned-away vehicles turned out to have come in for work unrelated to high voltage, such as brakes, tires, air conditioning and routine maintenance. All the mechanics took a one-day basic safety training, and these jobs began to be accepted at all three branches.

One lift at the Merkez branch was set aside; 620,000 TL was spent on equipment instead of 1.35 million TL. Two volunteer mechanics went to the five-day training for 76,000 TL, gave a two-year stay commitment, and moved to the specialist mechanic pay rate. The total investment came to 696,000 TL. Three months later the garage accepts 58 hybrid and electric vehicles a month, bringing in 377,000 TL of extra monthly revenue; vehicles turned away fell to 9.

What to do in a similar situation, step by step

1First 24 hours

  • Ask the front desk what the 64 turned-away vehicles came in for. A car that comes in for brakes, tires, air conditioning or suspension is not the same as one that comes in with a battery fault.
  • Talk to the mechanics: where does the fear come from? A mechanic who doesn't know which part of the vehicle can be touched under what conditions rightly touches nothing.

2This week

  • To be able to accept the jobs unrelated to high voltage, have all the mechanics take one day of basic safety training: orange cables, the service disconnect, lifting the vehicle safely.
  • Don't equip all three branches at once. Make one branch the specialist branch; set up the equipment there and have the other two branches route high-voltage work to it.
  • Choose two mechanics who volunteer for the five-day training and put a two-year stay commitment in writing in exchange for the training cost. Give something in return for the commitment, too: a specialist mechanic's pay rate.
  • Call the customers you turned away. If the phone numbers of the roughly 245 owners who left the door in six months are recorded at the front desk, that list is the cheapest advertising there is.

3Lasting fix

  • Train at least one more mechanic every year; if the knowledge stays with one person, the investment walks out the door the day that person leaves.
  • Put together a special maintenance package for owners of hybrid vehicles whose warranty has ended; be the first stop for a customer leaving the authorized dealer.
  • Record the number of vehicles turned away every month. Lost work becomes an investment case only if it is counted.
The lesson

A customer you turn away at the door takes not just that vehicle but the other one at home too.
