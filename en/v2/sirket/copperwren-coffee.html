Copperwren Coffee

Food & Drink · Portland, USA · Founded 2014 · 41 employees · 4 locations, $2.6 million in annual revenue

A small coffee chain in Portland that sells its own roasted coffee at four locations. It does $2.6 million a year in revenue, and a tenth of that comes from wholesale bean sales to cafes and restaurants in the area. Twenty-nine of its 41 employees are baristas, and the roaster sits in the back of the first location.
