Tallowfield Software

Software · Leeds, United Kingdom · Founded 2014 · 46 employees · 3,800 subscribing businesses

We build booking, job-sheet, quoting and invoicing software for small businesses such as plumbers, electricians and landscape gardeners. About 3,800 businesses use it on a subscription, paying an average of 42 pounds a month; most of our customers are businesses of one to five people.
