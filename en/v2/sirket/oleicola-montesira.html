Oleícola Montesira

Food · Jaén, Spain · Founded 1994 · 38 employees · 412 farmer members, 2,400–3,100 tonnes of olive oil a year

An olive mill that presses the olives of 412 farmer members in the villages east of Jaén. Depending on the harvest it produces between 2,400 and 3,100 tonnes of olive oil a year, selling most of it in bulk to bottlers and part of it under its own brand to importers abroad.

640 disputes in one season: members don't trust the sample taken from their olives

Payment for olives is based on the oil content of a sample taken at delivery. The result arrives on paper three weeks later, and when a member disputes it, there is no sample left to show them.
