Wendrath Antriebstechnik

Manufacturing · Esslingen, Germany · Founded 1968 · 140 employees · 21 million euros in annual revenue

A family business run by the third generation, making gearboxes and drive components for packaging, textile and food machinery. With 140 employees it turns over about 21 million euros a year; most of its output is made to order for individual customers, in small batches.
