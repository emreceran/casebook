Havenwijk Logistiek

Logistics · Rotterdam, Netherlands · Founded 2003 · 96 employees · 42,000 square metres of warehouse, 28 tractor units

Handles storage, order picking and distribution around the port of Rotterdam; it collects importers' containers from the terminal, brings them to the warehouse and delivers the goods to buyers in the Netherlands, Belgium and western Germany. It has 96 employees, a 42,000 square metre warehouse and 28 tractor units.
