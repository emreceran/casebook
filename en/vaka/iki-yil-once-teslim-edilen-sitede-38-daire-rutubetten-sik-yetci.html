In a complex delivered two years ago, 38 apartments complain of damp

In the 120-apartment complex, complaints cluster around balconies and terraces. The subcontractor that did the waterproofing has closed down; residents are preparing a joint petition.

Atabey Yapı Construction · Antalya · 60 employees · About 180 homes a year, 3 construction sites

The problem

At the 120-apartment Çınarlı Evler complex that Atabey Yapı delivered two years ago, complaints piled up after the second winter: 38 apartments have damp on the walls, bubbling paint and mold. Of the complaints, 26 are around balcony doors and terraces. Residents are preparing a joint petition, and the complex's name is circulating in neighborhood groups.

The subcontractor that did the waterproofing closed down a year ago, so there is no one to turn to. Levent Karataş proposes repairing the complaining apartments one by one: an average of 85,000 TL per apartment, 3.23 million TL in total. In his view, some of the complaints are also user error; condensation forms in an apartment where the owner doesn't ventilate.

Hande Özkök says that at the sales office of the new project, two buyers asked for their deposits back because of this complex. In her view, if the problem is limited to 38 apartments, it is important to know that, and if it isn't, to find out immediately: an independent expert should inspect the entire building. Rüstem Atabey, however, thinks that looking for damp in all 120 apartments would be summoning a complaint that doesn't exist with one's own hands.

Suggested solution AI suggestion

1First 24 hours

  • Call the complex management and ask for a meeting before the petition arrives. The message is simple: we acknowledge the problem, we are having the cause examined by an independent expert, and the cost is ours.
  • Until the report comes, nobody should say “they don't ventilate.” Even in an apartment where it is true, said without evidence it will be heard as blame.

2This week

  • Have an independent waterproofing expert prepare a root cause report. If 26 of the 38 complaints are at the same spot, that is not 26 separate failures but a single detail error.
  • Have moisture measured in all 120 apartments. An apartment that isn't complaining today but whose wall has started to get wet will be next winter's complaint; repairing it now is both cheaper and better for your reputation.
  • Divide the problems into two groups: waterproofing failure that lets water in, and condensation. The repair and the party responsible are different for each.
  • Write the repair schedule block by block and post it at the complex entrance. An owner who knows their place in line waits; one who doesn't files a lawsuit.

3Lasting fix

  • Make a water test mandatory on balconies and terraces before any finish is laid: the waterproofing is left under water for two days, the floor below is checked, and a record is made.
  • Take a guarantee from the waterproofing subcontractor that is released not when the job ends but at the end of the warranty period.
  • At the end of the first and second winters after delivery, make a proactive inspection visit to all apartments.
The lesson

26 complaints from the same spot are not 26 failures but a single mistake; a repair made without finding the mistake is nothing more than paint.
