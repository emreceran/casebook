Marketplace commission up four points, and 70 percent of revenue is there

Commission rose from 14% to 18%. The extra monthly cost of 308,000 TL is more than half the company's monthly profit.

Tüyo Pet E-commerce · Istanbul · 22 employees · 14,000 orders a month

The problem

The marketplace where Tüyo Pet sells the most announced that it is raising its commission rate on pet products from 14% to 18%. The change takes effect at the start of the month.

Of the company's monthly revenue of 11 million TL, 7.7 million TL comes from the marketplaces. A four-point increase means 308,000 TL of extra cost a month. The company's monthly net profit is about 550,000 TL.

Founder Irmak Güven is considering raising prices, but competitors sell the same products and a price comparison is one click away. Melis Aktaş, on the other hand, wants to set aside an ad budget for the company's own site.

By the numbers

Monthly revenue 11 million TL, marketplace share 70%
Commission rising from 14% to 18%
Extra cost: 308,000 TL a month
Monthly net profit: about 550,000 TL (5%)
What would you do?

Would you raise prices, pull out of the marketplace, or move customers to your own site?

Suggested solution AI suggestion

1First 24 hours

  • Don't respond the same way across all products. Work out the contribution margin by product: what is left of the sale price after product cost, commission and shipping?
  • Most likely the heavy, low-priced products (like large litter bags) tip into loss at the new rate. Any decision made without knowing which products make money is a guess.

2This week

  • For loss-making products, apply one of three options: correct the price, split the shipping burden by making multi-packs, or shut off sales on the marketplace.
  • Invite the customers who come from the marketplace to your own site: put a discount card for a second order in the package. The marketplace brings the customer once, and the later orders can be yours.
  • Food and litter are products consumed regularly. Launch a subscription on your own site: automatic shipment every month, 10% off. Giving half the commission to the customer is better than giving all of it to the marketplace.

3Lasting fix

  • Set a target for your own site's share of revenue (from 30% to 45% within a year) and divide the marketing budget accordingly.
  • Develop products you can't find anywhere else: private-label litter, your own packs. A product that can't be compared can pass a commission increase on in its price.
  • Set a ceiling on the share of any single channel. On a channel where someone else sets the rules, you're a tenant, not the landlord.
The lesson

A marketplace is a good place to find customers, not to keep them.
