Cat litter bursts in shipping: return rate rose from 4% to 11%

Switching to a cheaper shipping company saves 54,000 TL a month. Damaged deliveries are costing 71,000 TL.

Tüyo Pet E-commerce · Istanbul · 22 employees · 14,000 orders a month

The problem

Two months ago Tüyo Pet switched to a shipping company offering a price 18% lower per dimensional weight unit. Monthly savings are about 54,000 TL. In the same period, the rate of damaged deliveries and returns on cat litter orders rose from 4% to 11%.

About 2,600 litter orders go out each month. Each damaged order costs about 390 TL counting the product, shipping both ways and customer service labor. The store rating also slipped from 4.6 to 4.2.

Baran Çelik says the problem is with the shipping company. The shipping company argues the packaging is inadequate. Yiğit Polat's team spends every day replying to photos of burst bags.

By the numbers

Shipping savings: about 54,000 TL a month
Litter orders: 2,600 a month, damage rate from 4% to 11%
Cost per damaged order: about 390 TL
Extra damage cost: about 71,000 TL a month
What would you do?

Would you go back to the old shipping company, change the packaging, or both?

Suggested solution AI suggestion

1First 24 hours

  • Put the numbers side by side: 182 extra damaged orders a month at 390 TL comes to about 71,000 TL. The savings are 54,000 TL. Even without counting the rating loss, the cheap shipping is losing 17,000 TL a month.
  • Break the damage down by product, weight and region. The problem is most likely concentrated not in every package but in bags over 10 kilograms.

2This week

  • Don't tie yourself to a single company: send heavy products with the old company, whose damage rate is low, and light products with the new one. Most of the savings are kept and most of the damage goes away.
  • Strengthen the packaging on heavy products: double-wall cartons, filler that keeps the bag from shifting inside the carton. Measure the result with a trial shipment of ten units.
  • Submit documented damage claims to the shipping company in bulk and make the damage rate a subject of the contract.

3Lasting fix

  • Make the shipping decision not on the price per dimensional weight unit but on the total cost of delivery: shipping fee plus damage, returns and customer service costs.
  • Define an automatic shipping rule by product weight class; don't leave the decision to the preference of whoever is in the warehouse that day.
  • Put a ceiling on the damage rate in shipping contracts, with a discount clause that applies when it is exceeded.
The lesson

The cheapest shipping is the one that delivers the package intact.
