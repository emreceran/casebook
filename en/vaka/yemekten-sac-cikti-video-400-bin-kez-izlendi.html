A hair in the food, and the video was watched 400,000 times

A video shot by a customer spread within 36 hours. The branch's weekend revenue fell by a quarter. Is the real problem on the plate, or in the answer at the table?

Fırın Sokak Food & Drink · Izmir · 120 employees · 6 branches, central kitchen

The problem

On Saturday evening at the Bornova branch, a customer told the waiter there was a hair in his casserole. The waiter's answer was “it happens, let me bring you a new one”; the bill was charged in full. The customer shared a video of the plate and the waiter's response.

The video reached 400,000 views in 36 hours. The branch's map rating fell from 4.5 to 4.1, and dozens of one-star reviews unrelated to the incident came in. On Sunday, the branch's revenue was down 25% from the previous week.

Marketing Lead Gizem Er suggests filing to have the video taken down. Founder Cem Arıkan has been silent for two days because he doesn't know what to say.

What would you do?

Would you stay quiet and wait for it to be forgotten, try to get the video taken down, or come out and speak?

Suggested solution AI suggestion

1First 24 hours

  • Don't try to get the video removed and don't go on the defensive; both blow the incident up. First reach the customer directly: an apology under the founder's name, with no excuses, and a refund of the bill.
  • Publish a short statement the same day: what happened, we acknowledge it, here is what we did today. Three sentences are enough; a long statement reads like a defense.
  • Carry out a kitchen hygiene inspection at the branch that day and show that you did it. Concrete steps are what make a promise believable.

2This week

  • Audit hairnet, glove and kitchen entry rules at all branches. A gap at one branch is very likely present at the others too.
  • Train the entire service team on the moment of a complaint. Four steps: apologize, take the plate away immediately, remove the item from the bill, call the shift lead to the table.
  • Answer the comments about the incident one by one, without using stock phrases.

3Lasting fix

  • Give waiters the authority to resolve a complaint at the table: up to a certain amount, they shouldn't have to ask anyone before taking an item off the bill.
  • Set up a monthly mystery shopper visit and a rule of answering every review within 24 hours.
  • Record complaints by branch and topic. A recurring topic is the herald of the next video.
The lesson

The crisis began not with the hair on the plate but with the answer “it happens.” A mistake is forgiven; indifference is not.
