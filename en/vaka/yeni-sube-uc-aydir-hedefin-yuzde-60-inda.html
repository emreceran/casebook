The new branch has been at 60% of target for three months

The sixth branch, with 9 million TL invested, is full at lunch and empty in the evening. Should it be given time, changed, or closed?

Fırın Sokak Food & Drink · Izmir · 120 employees · 6 branches, central kitchen

The problem

The sixth branch, which Fırın Sokak opened three months ago, was planned with a monthly revenue target of 3.2 million TL. The actual average is 1.9 million TL. The branch's break-even point is 2.6 million TL; in other words, it posts a loss every month.

The branch is in a business district. At lunchtime there is a line, and after 6:00 p.m. the dining room is almost empty. The other five branches are in residential neighborhoods and make their main revenue from family tables in the evening and on weekends.

The investment was 9 million TL and monthly rent is 310,000 TL. Cem Arıkan says “a new branch takes a year to settle in.” Nalan Yücel reminds him that waiting costs 300,000 to 400,000 TL every month.

By the numbers

Monthly target 3.2 million TL, actual 1.9 million TL
Break-even point: 2.6 million TL
Investment: 9 million TL, rent: 310,000 TL a month
Lunch service is full, evening service is empty
What would you do?

Would you be patient for a year, adapt the branch to its area, or cut your losses and leave?

Suggested solution AI suggestion

1First 24 hours

  • Before passing judgment, break sales down by hour and day. “The branch didn't take off” and “the branch only works at lunch” are two very different diagnoses, and the solutions differ too.
  • Check on the spot whether there is an evening population in the area. If the street is empty in the evening, you can't create evening revenue with advertising.

2This week

  • Grow the hour where you're strong: a lunch menu ready in 15 minutes, pre-ordering to speed up the line, bulk and subscription lunches for nearby offices.
  • Cut the cost of the hour where you're weak: shrink the evening shift, move to a short menu, or close the branch at 7:00 p.m. You don't have to force the other branches' pattern onto this one.
  • Talk to the landlord about converting the rent to a revenue-linked model; an empty shop doesn't suit him either.

3Lasting fix

  • Write the decision threshold today: if monthly revenue doesn't reach 2.6 million TL within 90 days, the branch is handed over or scaled down. If the threshold isn't written in advance, every month someone will say, “let's wait a little longer.”
  • Leave the 9 million TL already spent out of the decision. That money is spent in every scenario; the only question is whether each month from here on pays its way.
  • Prepare a site selection checklist for future branches: daytime and evening population, weekend foot traffic, similarity to the customer profile of existing branches.
The lesson

Moving a successful model to the wrong place can cost more than a bad model, because nobody suspects the model.
